## Business Profile
AbbVie Inc. is a global research-based biopharmaceutical company focused on developing advanced therapies in immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and aesthetics (via Allergan). The company commercializes blockbuster immunology therapies Skyrizi (risankizumab) and Rinvoq (upadacitinib), oncology treatments Imbruvica and Venclexta, neuroscience medicines (Vraylar, Qulipta), and the iconic Botox aesthetic franchise.

AbbVie has successfully executed one of the biopharmaceutical industry's most impressive patent cliff transitions, replacing revenue from legacy blockbuster Humira with rapid growth in Skyrizi and Rinvoq across plaque psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, Crohn's disease, and ulcerative colitis. Management is advancing a deep oncology and neuroscience pipeline while returning substantial cash to shareholders via high-yield dividend growth.

AbbVie's economic moat is secured by robust patent portfolios, proven clinical superiority in immunology, and unmatched commercial salesforce execution.
