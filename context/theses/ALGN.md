## Business Profile
Align Technology, Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets the Invisalign system, the world's leading clear aligner orthodontic treatment, as well as iTero intraoral digital scanners and exocad CAD/CAM software for dental laboratories. The company provides a complete end-to-end digital orthodontic workflow connecting orthodontists, general dentists, and patients worldwide.

Align's competitive moat is founded on a proprietary database of over 18 million treated patients, sophisticated biomechanical 3D software algorithms, automated mass-customization manufacturing facilities, and unmatched global doctor adoption. Management is expanding orthodontic penetration in teen and complex malocclusion segments, driving digital scanner adoption in restorative dentistry, and generating high returns on invested capital with strong cash conversion.
