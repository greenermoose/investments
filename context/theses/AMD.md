## Core Investment Thesis
Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) operates as a leading player within the Information Technology sector (Semiconductors). Develops high-performance computing, graphics, and visualization technologies including EPYC CPUs, Ryzen, and Instinct AI accelerators. The company benefits from an established economic moat (EPYC server CPU market share momentum vs Intel, chiplet manufacturing architecture, open ROCm software stack.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.
