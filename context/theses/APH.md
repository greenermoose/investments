## Business Profile
Amphenol Corporation is one of the world's largest designers and manufacturers of electrical, electronic, and fiber optic connectors, coaxial and flat-ribbon cables, interconnect systems, and specialized sensors. The company serves diverse end markets including IT and data communications (AI data center server interconnects), automotive electronics, commercial aerospace, defense, industrial automation, and broadband communications.

Amphenol operates an agile, decentralized entrepreneurial business model comprising hundreds of operating units that rapidly innovate customized interconnect products for global OEM clients. Management is heavily levered to the surging demand for high-speed copper and optical interconnect solutions required to connect GPU clusters in AI computing architectures, while compounding capital through disciplined, bolt-on acquisitions.

Amphenol's decentralized engineering culture enables rapid time-to-market and high customer intimacy, generating consistent free cash flow and sustaining return on invested capital above 20%.
