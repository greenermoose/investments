## Core Investment Thesis
The Boeing Company (BA) operates as a leading player within the Industrials sector (Aerospace & Defense). Designs, manufactures, and services commercial jetliners, defense aircraft, satellites, and space communication systems. The company benefits from an established economic moat (Commercial aerospace duopoly with Airbus; however, heavily impaired by persistent manufacturing quality issues, debt burden, and regulatory production caps.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Structural Thesis Invalidation:** FAA production rate restrictions on 737 MAX sustained indefinitely, continued negative free cash flow burn, or further dilutive equity raises.
2. **Margin Deterioration:** Operating margins compress by more than 400 basis points across two consecutive quarters.
3. **Customer Retention / Churn Risk:** Unanticipated loss of key tier-one customers or sharp decline in net retention rates.
4. **Governance or Solvency Failure:** Material debt refinancing hurdles or unaddressed regulatory enforcement actions.
