## Business Profile
BlackRock, Inc. is the world's largest investment management firm, managing over $10 trillion in client assets across equity, fixed income, multi-asset, alternatives, and cash management strategies. The company serves institutional and retail clients worldwide through its dominant iShares exchange-traded fund (ETF) platform and active investment strategies, while providing Aladdin, the institutional standard enterprise risk management and investment operations software system.

BlackRock generates revenues primarily from recurring base management and securities lending fees scaled as a percentage of assets under management (AUM), alongside high-margin technology subscription revenue from Aladdin. Management is expanding into high-margin private markets and infrastructure assets (Global Infrastructure Partners acquisition) and digital asset ETFs, driving steady organic base fee growth.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
BlackRock possesses an extraordinary scale and technology moat anchored by its iShares ETF liquidity and Aladdin software integration. In exchange-traded funds, iShares benefits from tight bid-ask spreads, immense trading volume, and low expense ratios that create self-reinforcing liquidity flywheels. Meanwhile, Aladdin manages portfolio risk for hundreds of global financial institutions managing tens of trillions in assets, embedding massive software switching costs and providing BlackRock with durable recurring tech revenue.
