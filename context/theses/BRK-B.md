## Core Investment Thesis
Berkshire Hathaway is the quintessential defensive compounding engine. The company combines world-class property & casualty insurance underwriting (GEICO, Berkshire Reinsurance, General Re), essential infrastructure assets (BNSF Railway, Berkshire Hathaway Energy), and extensive manufacturing and retail businesses (Pilot, Precision Castparts, Lubrizol, Marmon).

A primary competitive advantage is Berkshire's massive insurance float ($170B+), which provides negative-cost permanent capital for capital allocation. Furthermore, Berkshire holds a record fortress cash reserve (> $250B in short-term US Treasury Bills), earning robust risk-free yield while maintaining complete optionality to deploy hundreds of billions into opportunistic acquisitions during market dislocations.

Underwriting discipline and a decentralized operating culture ensure long-term downside protection with market-beating risk-adjusted returns.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Destructive Capital Allocation:** Large value-destructive acquisition with significant goodwill write-downs post-succession.
2. **Structural Insurance Underwriting Losses:** Combined ratio consistently exceeding 105% over multiple years across primary insurance groups.
3. **Loss of Decentralized Autonomous Culture:** Invasive centralized managerial bureaucracy damaging operating business returns.
