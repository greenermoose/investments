## Business Profile
Berkshire Hathaway Inc. is a premier diversified conglomerate holding company led by disciplined value allocators, managing a collection of wholly owned operating businesses alongside a massive equity investment portfolio. Operating subsidiaries span property and casualty insurance (GEICO, Berkshire Hathaway Reinsurance), freight rail transportation (BNSF Railway), regulated utility and renewable energy (Berkshire Hathaway Energy), and manufacturing, services, and retail businesses (McLane, Precision Castparts, Lubrizol).

Berkshire's economic moat is generated by its immense insurance float (providing low-cost, permanent investment capital), fortress balance sheet with over $300 billion in cash and Treasury liquidity, and decentralized operating structure that attracts high-quality corporate acquisitions. Management deploys capital with strict hurdle discipline into high-ROIC operating assets, long-term public equity stakes, and opportunistic share repurchases.
