## Business Profile
CME Group Inc. is the world's largest and most diverse financial derivatives exchange, operating marketplaces for trading futures and options across benchmark asset classes including interest rates (SOFR, US Treasuries), equity indexes (S&P 500, Nasdaq-100), foreign exchange, energy, agricultural commodities, and metals. The company operates the Chicago Mercantile Exchange, Chicago Board of Trade, New York Mercantile Exchange, and The Commodity Exchange.

CME Group possesses one of the widest economic moats in global finance, powered by liquidity-driven network effects and centralized clearing house operations (CME Clearing). Market participants concentrate trading volume where open interest and liquidity are deepest to minimize execution slippage, creating self-reinforcing monopolies. With operating margins exceeding 65% and asset-light operations, CME converts substantial cash flow into shareholder returns via base and special dividends.
