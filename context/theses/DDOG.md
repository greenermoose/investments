## Business Profile
Datadog, Inc. is the leading cloud-native observability and security platform for modern cloud applications, integrating infrastructure monitoring, application performance monitoring (APM), log management, real user monitoring, and cloud security into a unified, automated interface. Datadog monetizes through tiered usage- and host-based SaaS subscription contracts.

Datadog benefits from strong customer workflow integration, rapid multi-product cross-selling (over 80% of customers use two or more modules), and continuous product innovation. Management is infusing generative AI tooling (Bits AI assistant) across its monitoring suite and capturing new observability workloads generated by enterprise AI application deployments.
