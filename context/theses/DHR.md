## Business Profile
Danaher Corporation is a global life sciences and diagnostics innovator designing, manufacturing, and marketing professional, medical, and commercial instruments, consumables, and services. Following the successful spin-offs of Fortive, Envista, and Veralto, Danaher operates as a focused biopharma and healthcare pure-play across three segments: Biotechnology (Cytiva bioprocessing equipment and filtration consumables), Life Sciences, and Diagnostics (Beckman Coulter, Cepheid).

The foundation of Danaher's operational excellence is the Danaher Business System (DBS), a proprietary continuous-improvement methodology rooted in lean manufacturing and capital allocation rigor. Management is levered to the long-term secular expansion of biologic and cell/gene therapy drug development, generating recurring revenue where over 80% of sales are high-margin consumables.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
Danaher commands an exceptional wide economic moat derived from high regulatory switching costs and mission-critical bioprocessing consumable specification. In biopharmaceutical manufacturing, Cytiva's chromatography resins, single-use bioreactors, and filtration membranes are explicitly specified in FDA biologic drug approval master files. Drug manufacturers cannot switch consumable suppliers without undergoing expensive, multi-year regulatory revalidation, locking in Danaher's recurring cash flow.
