## Business Profile
Dynatrace, Inc. provides a unified, AI-powered observability and application security platform designed for complex, hybrid multi-cloud enterprise computing environments. The Dynatrace platform ingests billions of system metrics, logs, traces, and user interactions in real time, delivering automated root-cause analysis, infrastructure monitoring, application performance management (APM), and runtime vulnerability analytics.

Dynatrace benefits from deep technical moats powered by its proprietary causal AI engine (Davis AI) and Grail data lakehouse architecture, which deliver precise automated problem resolution rather than simple statistical correlation. High enterprise switching costs, deep integration with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, and expanding adoption of Dynatrace Application Security drive net expansion rates above 110% and industry-leading free cash flow margins exceeding 28%.
