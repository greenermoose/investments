## Business Profile
EMCOR Group, Inc. is a Fortune 500 leader in mechanical and electrical construction, industrial infrastructure, and facilities services. The company designs, installs, and maintains complex electrical distribution systems, high-efficiency HVAC networks, cleanrooms, and fire protection systems for mission-critical facilities, including semiconductor fabrication plants, hyperscale data centers, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and healthcare campuses.

EMCOR's competitive edge relies on specialized technical engineering expertise, local market density, and an exemplary jobsite safety record that qualifies it for the most demanding high-tech construction projects. Benefiting from powerful secular tailwinds in domestic semiconductor reshoring, EV battery plants, and AI data center buildouts, EMCOR consistently expands high-margin electrical construction backlog while generating strong free cash flow and maintaining a conservative balance sheet.
