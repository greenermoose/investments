## Business Profile
Eaton Corporation plc is a global intelligent power management company providing electrical components, systems, and industrial solutions that manage electrical, hydraulic, and mechanical power safely and efficiently. The company operates through Electrical Americas, Electrical Global, Aerospace, Vehicle, and eMobility segments, supplying switchgear, transformers, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), busways, and power distribution units.

Eaton is positioned at the exact convergence of secular mega-trends: AI data center power infrastructure, utility grid modernization, industrial onshoring/electrification, and commercial building decarbonization. Management is deploying targeted growth capital to expand high-voltage electrical manufacturing capacity, driving sustained double-digit organic order growth and expanding operating margins across its global electrical operations.
