## Business Profile
Edwards Lifesciences Corporation is the global leader in patient-focused medical innovations for structural heart disease and critical care monitoring. The company pioneered Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement (TAVR) with its flagship SAPIEN valve platform, allowing minimally invasive valve replacement without open-heart surgery, alongside innovative Transcatheter Mitral and Tricuspid Therapies (TMTT).

Edwards maintains a wide economic moat supported by multi-decade clinical trial datasets, extensive physician training networks, and robust intellectual property portfolios across structural heart interventions. Management continues to expand clinical indications for TAVR into asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis, scale its tricuspid repair and replacement portfolio (EVOQUE and PASCAL), and reinvest over 17% of sales into R&D to sustain technology leadership.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
Edwards Lifesciences possesses a wide economic moat anchored by decades of clinical trial data, unmatched physician training programs, and robust patent portfolios in transcatheter heart valve technology.
