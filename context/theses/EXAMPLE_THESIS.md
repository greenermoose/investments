## Core Investment Thesis
Boeing operates in an effective global duopoly with Airbus in commercial aerospace manufacturing. Global airline passenger traffic is on a multi-decade expansion trajectory, and Boeing's order backlog spans over 5,400 commercial aircraft (representing over 6 years of production capacity).

While recent supply chain disruptions and quality audits have depressed free cash flow and compressed multiples, the long-term fundamentals remain intact. As manufacturing rates normalize and new aircraft deliveries accelerate, Boeing will transition from heavy cash burn to substantial free cash flow generation ($7B–$10B annually by 2028), driving significant multiple expansion and deleveraging.

## Revenue Drivers Narrative
Boeing's 13-quarter revenue expansion is anchored by four fundamental vectors:
1. **737 MAX Assembly Normalization:** Production rate transitions from the current FAA-capped 38 aircraft/month to 44/month by 2026-Q4, reaching 52/month by 2027-Q4 as factory stability and FAA quality audits clear.
2. **787 Dreamliner & Widebody Momentum:** 787 production scales from 5/month to 10/month by 2027, backed by massive replacement orders from major international carriers across transatlantic and Asia-Pacific routes.
3. **Defense, Space & Security (BDS) Margin Recovery:** Fixed-price developmental contracts mature and roll off by 2027, restoring BDS operating margins to historical positive mid-single digits.
4. **Global Services High-Margin Growth:** Commercial fleet utilization increases post-delivery, generating high-margin parts, maintenance, and digital solutions revenue growing at 6-8% annually.

## Valuation & P/S Multiple Narrative
Boeing currently trades at a depressed Price-to-Sales (P/S) multiple of ~1.50x on depressed TTM revenue ($78B), compared to its 10-year normalized pre-crisis median of 2.10x–2.40x.
As annual revenue recovers toward $108B over the next 12 quarters and operating cash flow margins expand from breakeven to 9.5%, valuation will re-rate upward toward a 1.65x–1.85x P/S multiple.
This multiple expansion combined with 40%+ top-line recovery generates an expected 3-year annualized return (CAGR) of 21.4%, well exceeding the 20% system hurdle rate and supporting a decisive **BUY** rating.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Critical Regulatory Ban:** Permanent grounding or cancellation of the 737 MAX or 777X program by the FAA or EASA.
2. **Structural Market Share Loss:** Order backlog share drops below 35% relative to Airbus over 4 consecutive quarters.
3. **Severe Balance Sheet Impairment:** Highly dilutive emergency equity raise (> 25% share dilution) without clear path to cash flow positivity.
4. **Persistent Quality/Safety Failure:** Re-emergence of systemic structural assembly failures halting factory production for > 6 months.
