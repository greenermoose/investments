## Business Profile
Fair Isaac Corporation (FICO) is a leading analytics and decision management software company, world-renowned for the FICO Score, the standard measure of consumer credit risk in the United States utilized in more than 90% of US lending decisions. The company operates two segments: Scores (B2B and B2C credit risk scoring algorithms) and Software (FICO Platform for enterprise fraud detection, customer onboarding, and decision optimization).

FICO commands an exceptionally durable economic moat derived from industry standardization, deep regulatory integration across mortgage (GSEs Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac), auto lending, and credit cards, and virtually insurmountable network effects. The Scores business requires negligible capital expenditure, allowing FICO to implement disciplined score pricing while expanding its modern cloud FICO Platform across global financial institutions.
