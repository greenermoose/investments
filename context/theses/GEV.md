## Business Profile
GE Vernova Inc. is a purpose-built global energy equipment and services leader operating across three core segments: Power (heavy-duty gas turbines, steam turbines, nuclear, and hydro power generation), Wind (onshore and offshore wind turbines), and Electrification (grid orchestration software, high-voltage substations, and power conversion equipment).

GE Vernova occupies a central role in meeting the world's soaring electrical power demand driven by AI data centers, industrial electrification, and electric vehicles. The company benefits from an installed base of over 7,000 gas turbines generating approximately 25% of the world's electricity, which creates decades of high-margin, recurring long-term service agreements (LTSAs). Management is driving operational turnaround in Wind and expanding high-margin Electrification grid backlog.

Surging electricity demand from AI data centers, industrial reshoring, and electric grid electrification reinforces GE Vernova's multi-year power equipment backlog and pricing power.
