## Core Investment Thesis
Alphabet combines the premier digital advertising business (Google Search, YouTube), a rapidly accelerating and profitable hyperscale cloud business (Google Cloud Platform), world-class proprietary AI research (Google DeepMind, Gemini models), and cutting-edge custom silicon (TPU v5p and v6).

Contrary to early fears of search disintermediation, Google Search AI Overviews are expanding query monetization and user engagement. YouTube remains the global leader in video watch time, with YouTube Shorts and Subscription offerings driving high-margin recurring cash flows. Google Cloud operating margins continue to expand (>10%), providing a third powerful profit engine.

Alphabet's balance sheet boasts over $100B in cash and short-term investments with negligible debt, funding active share repurchases and a newly instituted dividend.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Search Query Share Deterioration:** Google US search query volume drops below 80% for three consecutive quarters due to alternative AI search engines.
2. **Severe Antitrust Disintegration:** Court-ordered forced structural divestiture of Android or Chrome without value-neutral capital return.
3. **Advertising Multiple Collapse:** Permanent deceleration of Search advertising revenue to low single digits.
