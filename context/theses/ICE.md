## Business Profile
Intercontinental Exchange, Inc. (ICE) is a premier global operator of regulated financial exchanges, clearing houses, and market data networks, operating across three core segments: Exchanges (including the New York Stock Exchange / NYSE, global energy contracts, and interest rate derivatives), Fixed Income & Data Services (pricing, indices, and analytics), and Mortgage Technology (end-to-end digital mortgage origination and servicing platforms).

ICE commands an entrenched competitive advantage through mission-critical financial market infrastructure, exclusive proprietary energy benchmarks (Brent crude), and extensive recurring data feeds. Its strategic expansion into digital mortgage technology (Encompass and Black Knight) establishes a digitized end-to-end workflow for US residential lending. Management consistently delivers operating margin expansion and strong free cash flow generation.
