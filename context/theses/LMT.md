## Business Profile
Lockheed Martin Corporation is the world's largest defense prime contractor, specializing in the research, design, development, and integration of advanced aerospace, defense, and security technology systems. The company operates four segments: Aeronautics (F-35 Lightning II, C-130 Hercules, F-16), Missiles and Fire Control (HIMARS, PAC-3, Javelin), Rotary and Mission Systems (Sikorsky helicopters, Aegis combat systems), and Space (military satellites, missile defense).

Lockheed Martin serves as the premier strategic defense partner to the US Department of Defense and allied sovereign nations. Its crown jewel program is the 5th-generation F-35 Joint Strike Fighter, which represents a multi-decade production and maintenance program expected to operate through 2070. Management manages a record multi-year defense backlog exceeding $160 billion, driving stable cash flow and reliable dividend compounding.
