## Core Investment Thesis
McDonald's Corporation (MCD) operates as a leading player within the Consumer Discretionary sector (Restaurants & Global Franchising). World's leading global foodservice retailer with over 41,000 locations in more than 100 countries, operating on a heavily franchised real estate ownership model. The company benefits from an established economic moat (Global real estate ownership, 95% franchised high-margin rent and royalty stream, tremendous supply chain scale, and digital app loyalty engagement.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Structural Thesis Invalidation:** Severe comparable sales contraction in major markets or inability to maintain value leadership perception among low-income consumers.
2. **Margin Deterioration:** Operating margins compress by more than 400 basis points across two consecutive quarters.
3. **Customer Retention / Churn Risk:** Unanticipated loss of key tier-one customers or sharp decline in net retention rates.
4. **Governance or Solvency Failure:** Material debt refinancing hurdles or unaddressed regulatory enforcement actions.
