## Business Profile
Moody's Corporation is a cornerstone provider of global credit ratings, research, and risk analysis through Moody's Investors Service (MIS), and integrated risk management data, analytics, and software solutions through Moody's Analytics (MA). MIS rates sovereign and corporate debt obligations across global capital markets, while MA provides banking and insurance risk solutions, KYC/AML compliance data, and economic research.

Moody's operates within a heavily protected global ratings duopoly (alongside S&P Global) mandated by regulatory frameworks, bond covenants, and institutional investment guidelines. This structural position confers exceptional pricing power, high operating margins above 45%, and high free cash flow conversion. Management is scaling recurring SaaS analytics revenues across compliance and climate risk, driving multi-year compounding.
