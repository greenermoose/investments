## Core Investment Thesis
Microsoft possesses an unmatched commercial moat across enterprise IT, cloud infrastructure (Azure), productivity software (Office 365 / Microsoft 365), cybersecurity, developer tools (GitHub), and enterprise AI orchestration.

Azure continues to outgrow competing hyperscalers, driven by structural enterprise workload migrations and rapid adoption of Azure OpenAI Service and customized AI foundry tools. Microsoft 365 Copilot is creating an entirely new high-margin software tier, adding $30/user/month to core enterprise seats.

Free cash flow margins exceed 33%, supported by fortress balance sheet liquidity, high returns on invested capital (>28%), and defensive recurring subscription contracts.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Cloud Deceleration:** Azure revenue growth decelerates below 18% YoY for two consecutive quarters without macroeconomic justification.
2. **Enterprise IT Displacement:** Open-source AI tools causing significant churn in commercial Office 365 or developer GitHub environments.
3. **Severe ROIC Compression:** Massive datacenter capex spending without corresponding AI revenue monetization, reducing ROIC below 18%.
