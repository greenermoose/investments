## Core Investment Thesis
NIKE, Inc. (NKE) operates as a leading player within the Consumer Discretionary sector (Athletic Footwear & Apparel). Designs, markets, and distributes authentic athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories for sports and fitness activities worldwide. The company benefits from an established economic moat (World's most recognizable sports brand; currently suffering from innovation lag, loss of running footwear market share to On Running and Hoka, and weak China demand.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Structural Thesis Invalidation:** Continued market share loss in performance running or further gross margin compression from promotional discounting.
2. **Margin Deterioration:** Operating margins compress by more than 400 basis points across two consecutive quarters.
3. **Customer Retention / Churn Risk:** Unanticipated loss of key tier-one customers or sharp decline in net retention rates.
4. **Governance or Solvency Failure:** Material debt refinancing hurdles or unaddressed regulatory enforcement actions.
