## Business Profile
Oracle Corporation is a global technology leader providing enterprise cloud infrastructure, autonomous database platforms, and cloud application suites. The business operates through four segments: Cloud and License (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure / OCI, Oracle Autonomous Database, ERP/HCM SaaS suites including Fusion and NetSuite), Hardware (engineered database systems), and Services. OCI delivers high-performance compute clusters, GPU supercomputing for AI training, and multi-cloud database interoperability.

Oracle's strategic expansion centers on its Gen 2 Cloud Infrastructure, which provides RDMA networking and cost-efficient bare-metal GPU clusters tailored for generative AI workloads, winning multi-billion dollar infrastructure contracts with OpenAI, Microsoft, and leading enterprise AI developers. Management is scaling cloud database services across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud while driving recurring subscription revenue and expanding high-margin cloud gross margins.
