## Core Investment Thesis
PepsiCo, Inc. (PEP) operates as a leading player within the Consumer Staples sector (Packaged Foods & Beverages). Global food and beverage leader with complementary brands across Frito-Lay, Quaker, Gatorade, Pepsi-Cola, and Tropicana. The company benefits from an established economic moat (Direct-Store-Delivery (DSD) logistics network, global snacking brand dominance (Doritos, Lay's), pricing power, 50+ consecutive years of dividend increases.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Structural Thesis Invalidation:** Structural consumer volume decline in North American convenience snacking.
2. **Margin Deterioration:** Operating margins compress by more than 400 basis points across two consecutive quarters.
3. **Customer Retention / Churn Risk:** Unanticipated loss of key tier-one customers or sharp decline in net retention rates.
4. **Governance or Solvency Failure:** Material debt refinancing hurdles or unaddressed regulatory enforcement actions.
