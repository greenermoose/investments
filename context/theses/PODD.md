## Business Profile
Insulet Corporation is a pioneering medical device company focused on improving the lives of people with insulin-dependent diabetes through its proprietary Omnipod automated insulin delivery system. Omnipod is the world's leading tubeless, disposable, wearable insulin pump, featuring continuous glucose monitor (CGM) integration and automated algorithmic insulin dosing via the Omnipod 5 platform.

Insulet's competitive moat is driven by superior patient experience, a recurring consumable revenue model (users replace pods every 72 hours), and extensive clinical trial evidence demonstrating improved glycemic control. By delivering Omnipod through the pharmacy benefit channel rather than complex durable medical equipment (DME) pathways, Insulet achieves rapid patient onboarding, high brand loyalty, and international market share expansion across both Type 1 and intensive Type 2 diabetes populations.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
Insulet commands a durable medical technology moat rooted in proprietary electromechanical micro-pump engineering, extensive patent protections, and superior patient adherence. Its flagship Omnipod 5 is the premier tubeless automated insulin delivery system, eliminating burdensome infusion tubing and integrating directly with continuous glucose monitors for automated insulin delivery. Delivering Omnipod via the pharmacy benefit channel rather than traditional durable medical equipment channels provides frictionless patient access, rapid recurring pod consumable reorders every 72 hours, and dominant market share in wearable diabetes tech.
