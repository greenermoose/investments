## Business Profile
Quanta Services, Inc. is the leading specialized contracting services provider delivering comprehensive infrastructure solutions for the electric power, renewable energy, and communications industries. Its capabilities include designing, constructing, and maintaining high-voltage transmission lines, electrical substations, utility-scale solar and wind generation, and modern fiber-optic communications networks.

Quanta commands an unmatched competitive advantage built on North America's largest skilled craft labor workforce, proprietary equipment fleet, and master service agreements (MSAs) with major investor-owned utilities. As electric utilities invest hundreds of billions to upgrade aging power grids, interconnect renewable energy, and satisfy data center power demand, Quanta's massive scale and safety track record make it the indispensable builder of the modern electric grid.
