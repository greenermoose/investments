## Core Investment Thesis
Ross Stores, Inc. (ROST) operates as a leading player within the Consumer Discretionary sector (Apparel & Home Merchandise Retail). Operates off-price retail apparel and home fashion stores under the Ross Dress for Less and dd's DISCOUNTS brands. The company benefits from an established economic moat (Opportunistic brand inventory sourcing network, lean store format, insulated from e-commerce competition via deep discount treasure-hunt model.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Structural Thesis Invalidation:** Supply chain cost inflation or failure to manage store inventory levels leading to markdowns.
2. **Margin Deterioration:** Operating margins compress by more than 400 basis points across two consecutive quarters.
3. **Customer Retention / Churn Risk:** Unanticipated loss of key tier-one customers or sharp decline in net retention rates.
4. **Governance or Solvency Failure:** Material debt refinancing hurdles or unaddressed regulatory enforcement actions.
