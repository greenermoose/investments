## Core Investment Thesis
Starbucks Corporation (SBUX) operates as a leading player within the Consumer Discretionary sector (Specialty Coffee Retail & Roasteries). World's premier roaster, marketer, and retailer of specialty coffee, operating over 38,000 company-operated and licensed stores globally. The company benefits from an established economic moat (Global premier specialty coffee brand, high customer stickiness via 34M+ US active Rewards members, premium real estate locations, and digital order-and-pay dominance.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Structural Thesis Invalidation:** Failure of 'Back to Starbucks' turnaround plan under Brian Niccol or prolonged negative comparable store sales in North America and China.
2. **Margin Deterioration:** Operating margins compress by more than 400 basis points across two consecutive quarters.
3. **Customer Retention / Churn Risk:** Unanticipated loss of key tier-one customers or sharp decline in net retention rates.
4. **Governance or Solvency Failure:** Material debt refinancing hurdles or unaddressed regulatory enforcement actions.
