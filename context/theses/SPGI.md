## Business Profile
S&P Global Inc. is the world's premier provider of transparent and independent credit ratings, financial benchmarks, commodity price assessments, and essential workflow intelligence to capital and commodity markets globally. The business operates five major divisions: S&P Global Ratings, S&P Global Market Intelligence (Capital IQ platform), S&P Dow Jones Indices (S&P 500, Dow Jones Industrial Average), S&P Global Mobility, and S&P Global Commodity Insights (Platts).

S&P Global monetizes through transaction fees on debt issuance ratings, recurring software and market data subscriptions, and asset-linked licensing fees on trillions of dollars indexed to the S&P 500 and Dow Jones benchmarks. Management is actively infusing AI into Capital IQ analytics, expanding private market intelligence, and delivering exceptional operating margins exceeding 45%.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
S&P Global commands one of the widest and most impenetrable economic moats in global finance, functioning as a federally registered Nationally Recognized Statistical Rating Organization (NRSRO) within an entrenched credit ratings duopoly alongside Moody's. Institutional bond mandates legally require independent ratings from S&P or Moody's for corporate debt to be eligible for pension fund purchase, granting S&P unmatched pricing power. Simultaneously, the S&P 500 index represents the global standard for equity benchmarking, generating perpetual asset-linked royalty streams.
