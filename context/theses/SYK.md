## Business Profile
Stryker Corporation is a premier global medical technology company operating through two primary segments: MedSurg and Neurotechnology (surgical equipment, navigation systems, endoscopic imaging, neurovascular devices) and Orthopaedics (joint replacement implants, trauma fixation, and spinal surgery systems).

Stryker's flagship technological platform is the Mako robotic-arm assisted surgery system, which enables orthopedic surgeons to execute total knee, partial knee, and total hip replacements with millimeter precision. Management drives organic revenue growth by expanding Mako robotic indications (including spine and shoulder applications), selling high-margin proprietary consumable surgical tools, and maintaining dominant hospital equipment share.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
Stryker maintains a formidable economic moat built on robotic platform lock-in (Mako), hospital procurement entrenchment, and surgeon switching costs. When a hospital system invests hundreds of thousands of dollars in a Mako robotic console, orthopedic surgeons exclusively utilize Stryker's proprietary implants and cutting blades for every procedure. This razor-and-blade model generates high-margin, recurring implant sales and reinforces Stryker's gross margins above 65%.
