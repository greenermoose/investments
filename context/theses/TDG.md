## Business Profile
TransDigm Group Incorporated is a leading global designer, producer, and supplier of highly engineered, proprietary aircraft components for commercial transport aircraft, regional jets, military aircraft, and defense systems. Its product catalog spans ignition systems, mechanical actuators, engine pumps, specialty valves, aerospace motors, and cockpit security devices.

TransDigm's economic moat is among the widest in the industrial sector: over 90% of net sales are generated from proprietary products for which TransDigm holds sole-source manufacturing rights, and roughly 75% of revenue is derived from high-margin aftermarket replacement parts. Because components are flight-critical and FAA-certified, airlines face immense regulatory hurdles to replace them, giving TransDigm unmatched pricing power, EBITDA margins exceeding 50%, and exceptional multi-decade compounding.
