## Business Profile
Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. is the world leader in serving science, providing an unparalleled breadth of analytical instruments, laboratory equipment, biopharma manufacturing consumables, clinical research services (PPD contract research organization), and specialty diagnostics to pharmaceutical, biotechnology, academic, and government laboratories worldwide.

Thermo Fisher operates as the indispensable 'one-stop shop' for life sciences research, leveraging its global supply chain and e-commerce distribution platforms to deliver everything from electron microscopes and mass spectrometers to sterile pipettes and clinical trial logistics. Management compounds capital through high-return R&D investments, disciplined M&A integration, and expanding high-margin biopharma services.
