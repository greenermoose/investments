## Business Profile
Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSMC) is the world's largest and most technologically advanced dedicated pure-play semiconductor foundry. The company manufactures advanced logic integrated circuits, high-performance computing (HPC) processors, AI accelerators, mobile system-on-chips (SoCs), and automotive silicon for leading global fabless semiconductor designers including NVIDIA, Apple, AMD, Qualcomm, and Broadcom.

TSMC operates the global semiconductor industry's most sophisticated manufacturing fabs, pioneering leading-edge process nodes (N3, N2, and A16 GAA transistor architectures) alongside advanced packaging solutions (CoWoS, InFO, and SoIC). Management is executing a strategic global manufacturing footprint expansion across Taiwan, the United States (Arizona), Japan (Kumamoto), and Europe (Dresden), capturing substantial secular growth from artificial intelligence computing infrastructure and high-performance silicon demand.
