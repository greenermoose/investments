## Business Profile
The Trade Desk, Inc. operates the world's leading independent cloud-based demand-side programmatic advertising platform (DSP). The platform enables ad agencies and enterprise brand marketers to plan, purchase, manage, and optimize data-driven digital advertising campaigns across connected TV (CTV), online video, audio, mobile, display, and digital out-of-home channels in real time.

The Trade Desk operates as a pure buy-side platform with no media ownership or inventory conflicts of interest, aligning completely with advertising buyers. Management is capitalizing on the massive migration of linear television advertising budgets to Connected TV, championing the open internet identity standard Unified ID 2.0 (UID2), and leveraging its AI media optimization engine, Kokai, to drive programmatic efficiency.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
The Trade Desk possesses a robust economic moat driven by strong data network effects, independent buy-side alignment, and massive platform scale processing over one trillion ad queries daily. Because The Trade Desk does not own digital media inventory (unlike closed gardens Google and Meta), major media publishers and streaming networks (Disney, Netflix, Paramount) willingly share first-party data and CTV inventory. This independence attracts top global brands, creating high client retention consistently exceeding 95% and operating margins above 35%.
