## Business Profile
Uber Technologies, Inc. is the world's premier technology platform for on-demand mobility, delivery, and freight logistics, connecting hundreds of millions of consumers with independent drivers and merchant partners across 70+ countries. The business operates three primary segments: Mobility (ridesharing, taxi dispatch, and corporate commute), Delivery (Uber Eats restaurant food, grocery, and convenience retail delivery), and Freight (digital logistics brokerage).

Uber operates a capital-light platform model monetizing through take-rates on gross bookings across its global marketplace. Management has achieved sustained GAAP operating profitability and significant free cash flow generation by expanding high-margin advertising networks (Uber Advertising), cross-selling between Mobility and Delivery through the Uber One membership program, and partnering with autonomous vehicle developers (Waymo, Cruise) to deploy autonomous fleets at scale.

## Competitive Moat Analysis
Uber commands a powerful two-sided local network effect across global metropolitan markets, pairing driver supply density with massive consumer demand to minimize rider wait times and maximize driver utilization. High platform liquidity creates high customer conversion and allows Uber to cross-sell food delivery to rideshare users at near-zero customer acquisition cost. Furthermore, Uber's membership program (Uber One) drives higher frequency, lower churn, and high-margin advertising monetization that subscale regional competitors cannot match.
