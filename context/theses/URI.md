## Business Profile
United Rentals, Inc. is the world's largest equipment rental company, operating a comprehensive network of over 1,500 rental locations across North America, Europe, and Australia. The company serves commercial construction, industrial manufacturing, infrastructure, and event customers, offering an extensive fleet of general construction equipment (aerial work platforms, earthmoving, material handling) and specialty equipment (power and HVAC, trench safety, fluid solutions).

United Rentals maintains an insurmountable scale advantage over fragmented local competitors, utilizing proprietary digital telematics (Total Control software) and dynamic fleet repositioning to maximize equipment utilization and return on invested capital. Its Specialty rental division provides high-margin, recession-resilient revenues, while substantial free cash flow is redeployed into accretive tuck-in acquisitions and share repurchases.
