## Business Profile
Veeva Systems Inc. is the leading global provider of cloud-based software, data, and analytics solutions purpose-built for the life sciences and pharmaceutical industries. Its core platform offerings include Veeva Development Cloud (unifying clinical trial operations, regulatory compliance, quality control, and safety data across drug development) and Veeva Commercial Cloud (delivering omnichannel CRM, medical affairs management, and commercial content).

Veeva commands a near-monopoly industry cloud moat characterized by massive regulatory compliance switching costs, high gross margins above 70%, and a pristine, zero-debt balance sheet with robust net cash reserves. With life sciences enterprises standardized on Veeva Vault as their single source of regulatory truth, management is expanding into clinical data management (Veeva Vault EDC), proprietary industry data feeds (Veeva Compass), and AI-driven commercial insights, sustaining durable double-digit top-line compounding.
