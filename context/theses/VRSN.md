## Business Profile
VeriSign, Inc. is the global provider of internet domain name registry services and internet infrastructure, exclusively operating the definitive registry for the world's two largest top-level domain (TLD) extensions: .com and .net. The company also operates two of the world's 13 root zone domain name system (DNS) servers, ensuring the uninterrupted routing and navigational security of the global internet.

VeriSign operates an absolute legal and infrastructural monopoly under exclusive agreements with ICANN and the US Department of Commerce. With near-zero incremental marginal costs for domain registrations and contractual annual price increase provisions, VeriSign delivers extraordinary gross margins approaching 88%, operating margins above 67%, and near-100% free cash flow conversion, which management systematically returns to shareholders through massive share buybacks.
