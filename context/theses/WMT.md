## Core Investment Thesis
Walmart Inc. (WMT) operates as a leading player within the Consumer Staples sector (Omnichannel Hypermarket Retail). Engages in retail and wholesale operations, operating grocery and merchandise stores, hypermarkets, and e-commerce platforms across the US and international markets. The company benefits from an established economic moat (Unmatched physical store proximity (within 10 miles of 90% of US population), massive grocery purchasing power, fast-growing high-margin retail media advertising (Walmart Connect), and automation.), positioning it to generate sustainable cash flows and attractive risk-adjusted returns across the 3-to-5 year horizon.

## Explicit Invalidation Criteria (Exit Triggers)
If any of the following occur, the thesis is broken and the position will be exited:
1. **Structural Thesis Invalidation:** Operating margin compression below 3.5% or severe loss of market share in grocery to discounters.
2. **Margin Deterioration:** Operating margins compress by more than 400 basis points across two consecutive quarters.
3. **Customer Retention / Churn Risk:** Unanticipated loss of key tier-one customers or sharp decline in net retention rates.
4. **Governance or Solvency Failure:** Material debt refinancing hurdles or unaddressed regulatory enforcement actions.
