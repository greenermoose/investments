Strategy Parameters & Target Metrics

Cash-Secured Puts (CSPs)

Delta (Δ): 0.15 to 0.30 (80%–85% probability of profit).

DTE: 30 to 45 Days to Expiration.

Hurdle Rate: Minimum 12% to 18% Annualized Return on Collateral (AROC).

Collateral: 100% reserved in SGOV or cash.

Covered Calls (CCs)

Eligibility: Only on equity lots with ≥ 100 shares.

Strike: Out-of-the-money (OTM) above cost basis, aligned with fair value target.

Delta (Δ): 0.20 to 0.35.

DTE: 21 to 45 Days to Expiration.

Annualized Return on Collateral (AROC) Formula

AROC = ( Option Premium Strike Price ) × ( 365 DTE )

For example, selling a $100 strike put with 35 DTE for $1.80 generates:

AROC = ( $1.80 $100.00 ) × ( 365 35 ) = 1.80% × 10.428 = 18.77% Annualized Return

Weekend Black-Scholes Modeling

Because options markets are closed over the weekend, the Pricing Agent calculates theoretical fair values using closed prices and volatility surface data via the Black-Scholes analytical model:

Call Option Price : C(S, t) = S · N(d1) − Ke−r(T−t) · N(d2)
Put Option Price : P(S, t) = Ke−r(T−t) · N(−d2) − S · N(−d1)
where d1 = ln(S / K) + (r + σ2 / 2)(T − t) σ √(T − t) ,   d2 = d1 − σ √(T − t)
  • Underlying Price (S): Friday 4:00 PM ET closing price.
  • Strike Price (K): Candidate strike price.
  • Risk-Free Rate (r): Current 3-Month US Treasury yield (FRED / yfinance).
  • Implied Volatility (σ): 30-day historical and implied volatility percentile.
  • Time to Expiration (T − t): Days to Expiration (DTE) scaled in years (DTE / 365).
  • Cumulative Standard Normal Distribution (N): Probability distribution function for delta and exercise bounds.
  • Limit Order Setting: Modeled fair values are used to specify Monday 9:30 AM ET limit prices that enforce the stated experimental reservation-price floor.

Rolling & Buy-to-Close Mechanics

The Net-Credit Roll Rule

When rolling options on thesis-intact holdings (e.g. threatened in-the-money CSPs or expiring CCs), positions are rolled out in time and away in strike strictly for a net credit. Never pay a debit to roll an options position.

Buy to Close (BTC) on Losing Propositions

When an underlying equity's thesis breaks or is deemed a losing proposition, we execute BUY TO CLOSE limit orders on Monday open:

  • Short Puts: Closes the put contract to avert assignment, eliminating the downside risk of being forced to buy a stock going further down.
  • Short Calls: Closes the short call to release the collateral lock, enabling immediate Monday open liquidation of underlying shares.
Capital Preservation Over Premium Retention

Accepting a contained debit to buy to close a short put or call is far superior to taking assignment on a declining value trap or holding locked shares during a downward spiral. The system decisively prioritizes multi-decade compounding and downside defense.
