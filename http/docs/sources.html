Authoritative Ingestion & Ground-Truth Verification

All financial statements, share counts, and macro rates are ground-truthed against Tier 1 SEC EDGAR and Federal Reserve feeds. Market prices and Wall Street analyst coverage are tracked through verified secondary aggregators linking directly to audited URLs. Explore our active universe on the Public Equities portal.

Detailed Classification of Key Data Aggregators

1. Yahoo Finance (finance.yahoo.com)

Classification: Tier 2 Secondary Market Data & News Aggregator.
Assessment: High for historical prices, trading volume, and split/dividend adjustments. Moderate for consensus estimates.
Role in Due Diligence: Serves as a rapid, zero-friction engine for pulling daily and multi-month OHLCV candlestick series, calculating 20-day and 50-day simple moving averages, and establishing 52-week support/resistance bands.
Technical Caveat: Lacks an official developer REST API; internal endpoints change cookie/crumb headers. Ideal for ad-hoc inspection rather than the sole production backbone.

2. TipRanks (tipranks.com)

Classification: Tier 2 Analyst Accountability & Sentiment Aggregator.
Assessment: High for individual sell-side analyst track records, historical win rates, and price target revisions.
Role in Due Diligence: Provides analyst scoring by measuring the historical success rate and average return of specific Wall Street analysts (e.g. Dan Ives at Wedbush, Toni Sacconaghi at Bernstein, Toshiya Hari at Goldman Sachs).
Strategic Utility: Enables AI agents to weigh analyst price targets based on proven forecasting accuracy on that specific equity.

3. StockAnalysis.com (stockanalysis.com)

Classification: Tier 2 Secondary Fundamental Screener & Financial Portal.
Assessment: Moderate to High. Offers clean, modern tabular summaries of multi-year financial statements, valuation multiples, and dividend histories.
Role in Due Diligence: Fast visual and tabular cross-check for multi-year revenue trajectories, gross margin expansion, free cash flow generation, and consensus estimates.
Verification Rule: As derived secondary data, critical figures must be cross-checked against Tier 1 SEC EDGAR XBRL filings.

4. MarketBeat (marketbeat.com)

Classification: Tier 2 Wall Street Analyst Price Target & Rating Aggregator.
Assessment: High for verified rating changes, price target revisions, and link-level auditability.
Role in Due Diligence: Primary ground-truthed public aggregator for tracking individual analyst price targets. Publishes dedicated time-stamped tables linking directly to verifiable URLs where analyst name, firm, date, and target price are confirmed.

5. Morningstar (morningstar.com)

Classification: Tier 2 Institutional Fundamental Research & Economic Moat Authority.
Assessment: Very High for qualitative competitive moat analysis, capital allocation stewardship, and discounted cash flow (DCF) fair value modeling.
Role in Due Diligence: Gold-standard framework for evaluating structural competitive advantages (network effects, switching costs, cost advantages, intangible assets, efficient scale) and independent DCF fair value benchmarks.

6. Corporate Investor Relations (IR) Portals

Classification: Tier 1 / Tier 2 Primary Corporate Disclosures & Issuer Communications.
Assessment: High (9.2/10) for official earnings decks, webcast transcripts, and multi-year roadmaps. Unaudited non-GAAP claims require SEC GAAP reconciliation.
Role in Due Diligence: Delivers high-conviction context complementing SEC filings: segment KPIs (ARR, net retention, ARPU), TAM expansion targets, management guidance, and catalyst timelines.
Integration: Directly linked per equity in universe.json and accessible with one click in the Public Equities inspection modal.

How Sell-Side Analyst Reports & Price Targets Are Tracked and Discovered

The Institutional Research Ecosystem

Primary sell-side equity research reports are proprietary 10-to-50 page dossiers authored by chartered analysts at major global investment banks (Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Bernstein, Wedbush, Bank of America, UBS, Barclays, Citigroup, Jefferies, Evercore ISI, Baird). These reports are commercial intellectual property distributed to paying institutional clients behind enterprise terminal paywalls (Bloomberg Professional Terminal, FactSet, LSEG Workspace / Refinitiv, S&P Capital IQ).

How Price Targets Become Public

Although full PDF reports remain behind client paywalls, the headline metrics and revisions are disseminated immediately through three public and secondary channels:

1. Morning Wires

Pre-Market Institutional Releases (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM ET)

Investment banks issue executive wire releases announcing rating changes, target revisions, and thesis summaries. Financial newswires (Bloomberg News, Reuters, Dow Jones, The Fly on the Wall, StreetInsider, Benzinga, Seeking Alpha) publish immediate dispatches containing analyst names, firms, old/new price targets, and key drivers.

2. Structured Aggregators

Aggregator Ingestion & API Feeds

Structured aggregators (MarketBeat, TipRanks, Financial Modeling Prep API, Yahoo Finance) ingest newswire dispatches into normalized databases. For example, the FMP API provides dedicated endpoints (GET /v4/price-target?symbol=AAPL) returning analyst names, firms, price targets, announcement dates, and news links.

3. Corporate Disclosures

SEC 8-K Filings & Investor Relations Fact Sheets

Public corporations regularly disclose sell-side coverage rosters, consensus targets, and analyst day presentations via SEC EDGAR and Investor Relations (IR) websites.

Search Engines & Live Ingestion Tools for AI Agents

AI agents utilize three specialized tools to conduct real-time market research and catalyst investigations:

1. search_web Engine

Programmatic interface to global search indexes (Google / Bing). Enables agents to execute targeted, disambiguated queries across corporate investor relations portals, regulatory agency announcements (FDA, FAA, FTC), and breaking macroeconomic briefings.

2. read_url_content & Browser Subagent

HTTP content fetchers and headless browser subagents capable of rendering static markdown, parsing HTML DOMs, and interacting with dynamic JavaScript applications to extract earnings call transcripts, CEO interviews, and SEC exhibits.

3. SEC EDGAR Full-Text Search (EFTS) API

The SEC's native full-text search engine (https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index) indexing every word across millions of regulatory filings since 2001. Allows agents to search litigation disclosures, customer concentration figures, and supply chain dependencies directly inside 10-K/10-Q footnotes.

How AI Agents Generate Knowledge from Internal Weights (Parametric Knowledge)

A central capability of foundation Large Language Models (LLMs) is synthesizing sophisticated qualitative analyses, competitive dynamics, financial theories, and business moat assessments directly from internal neural network weights without invoking external tools. Here is how this mathematical process operates:

Step 1

Pre-Training: Compressing Market Intelligence into High-Dimensional Weights

During pre-training on supercomputing clusters, the model processes trillions of tokens spanning decades of SEC 10-K/10-Q filings, shareholder letters, financial newswires, earnings transcripts, academic finance literature (Fama-French, Black-Scholes), and corporate case studies. Through self-supervised autoregressive learning, the model adjusts billions of neural weight parameters across hundreds of transformer layers, creating a high-dimensional latent representation of accounting relationships, operating leverage, and competitive strategy.

Step 2

Autoregressive Next-Token Generation: From Weights to Actionable Analysis

When prompted to analyze an equity, the generation process executes mathematically:

  • Context Encoding: The prompt, instructions, guidelines, and conversation history are converted into input token embeddings.
  • Multi-Head Self-Attention: Transformer layers compute dynamic attention weights across concepts, activating neural pathways associated with pricing power, switching costs, or option Greek calculations.
  • Feed-Forward Knowledge Projection: Dense layers project hidden states through weight matrices encoding deep statistical regularities about business models.
  • Logit Computation & Vocabulary Sampling: Output projection layers generate logits across the vocabulary (~32,000 to 256,000 tokens), converted by softmax into probability distributions from which the next token is generated.
  • Autoregressive Loop: Each token is appended to the context sequence, producing structured, coherent, institutional financial analysis.
Step 3

Post-Training Alignment (SFT & RLHF/DPO)

Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) align the token distribution toward rigorous step-by-step reasoning, institutional financial terminology, schema compliance, and objective risk analysis.

Tier 4 Parametric Governance Rules

Because internal weights have a static training cutoff and may probabilistically drift on precise decimal numbers, the system enforces three governance rules:

  1. Explicit Provenance: Tag internal knowledge as TIER_4_AGENT_PARAMETRIC_KNOWLEDGE.
  2. Runtime Context Signature: Record system clock timestamp, active role persona, and prompt context.
  3. Mandatory SEC Cross-Check: All critical historical financial figures and share counts generated from parametric memory must be verified against Tier 1 SEC EDGAR XBRL data.

Verification & Errata Correction Protocol

When any stored claim, financial figure, or assumption is identified as erroneous, stale, or hallucinated, the system executes an audited correction lifecycle:

  1. Detection: Flag conflicting data point against Tier 1 SEC EDGAR or direct exchange data.
  2. Triangulation: Retrieve official primary source evidence and verify ground truth value.
  3. In-Place Correction: Update the target dossier in context/theses/ or dataset with new citation.
  4. Errata Log Entry: Append a permanent audit record to context/research/errata_log.md matching context/schemas/errata_schema.json.
