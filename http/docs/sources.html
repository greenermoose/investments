Data Sources, Provenance & Verification

Institutional standards for authoritative data hierarchy, access methodologies, AI agent parametric knowledge attribution, and systematic errata correction.

Authoritative Ingestion & Mathematically Grounded Targets

Share prices and trading volumes are continuously verified via exchange market feeds (Tier 2 authority). Benchmark Entry Prices and Target Exit Prices are strictly grounded in actual market prices, 20-day technical support/resistance bands, and empirical 20%+ annualized CAGR compounding models rather than speculative estimates. Click any equity in Public Equities to inspect technical indicators, historical candlestick series, and SEC XBRL filings.

Authoritative Data Hierarchy

To eliminate hallucination and guarantee factual rigor, all quantitative metrics, catalyst dates, and financial figures follow a strict five-tier authority hierarchy:

Tier Source Classification Primary Providers & Formats Authority Level
Tier 1 Primary Regulatory & Exchanges SEC EDGAR (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, Form 4), CBOE, NYSE, NASDAQ Absolute Ground Truth
Tier 2 Institutional Aggregators & Analyst Feeds FRED, US Treasury, Yahoo Finance, FMP Price Targets, FactSet, LSEG High Reliability / Secondary
Tier 3 Quantitative Literature & Consensus CBOE Indices, AQR/Fama-French Studies, Wall Street Consensus Estimates Empirical Strategy & Consensus Baseline
Tier 4 AI Agent Parametric Knowledge Model Internal Weights (Pre-training, SFT, RL) + Context Signature Qualitative / Unverified Numbers
Tier 5 Private User Snapshot Brokerage Records in private/snapshots/ (CSV / Screenshots) Private Ground Truth

Wall Street Analyst Coverage & Price Targets

Individual sell-side analyst price targets and research notes are captured and tracked for all public equities in the active universe. Each recorded price target captures five core attributes:

1. Analyst Name & Firm

Lead equity research analyst issuing the report and their institutional firm (e.g. Toni Sacconaghi / Bernstein, Dan Ives / Wedbush, Toshiya Hari / Goldman Sachs).

2. Announcement Date

The exact date (YYYY-MM-DD) the price target or rating revision was published to institutional clients.

3. Stock Symbol

The primary exchange-listed ticker symbol (e.g. AAPL, NVDA, MSFT).

4. Market Price at Announcement

The prevailing market price of the common stock on the date of the research report release, establishing the initial return benchmark.

5. Target Price & Implied Upside

The analyst's 12-month forward price target per share and the resulting implied percentage appreciation or return.

6. Verifiable Source Link

A direct URL to the Tier 2 institutional aggregator record (e.g. MarketBeat) where the analyst name, firm, date, and price target can be audited.

AI Agent Parametric Knowledge & Context Signature

AI agents frequently synthesize insights from internal weights (pre-training, SFT, RL) without executing tool calls. This is fully supported, provided agents explicitly document the provenance and attach a runtime context signature.

1. Explicit Declaration

Tag internal knowledge as TIER_4_AGENT_PARAMETRIC_KNOWLEDGE and mark numerical assertions as unverified pending Tier 1 SEC cross-check.

2. System Clock Observation

Record the observed runtime system clock timestamp (e.g. 2026-08-16T11:15:00-04:00) to benchmark time-sensitive statements.

3. Role & Prompt Context

Describe the active operational role (e.g. Fundamental Analyst) and conversation prompt context governing the synthesis.

4. Model Identity Transparency

If base model architecture or exact training cutoff is not internally exposed, explicitly note that model metadata is context-inferred.

Verification & Errata Correction Protocol

When an analytical assumption, catalyst target, or historical figure is found to be erroneous or superseded, the system executes an audited correction lifecycle:

  1. Detection: Flag conflicting data point against Tier 1 SEC EDGAR or direct exchange data.
  2. Triangulation: Retrieve official primary source evidence and verify ground truth value.
  3. In-Place Correction: Update the target dossier in context/theses/ or dataset with new citation.
  4. Errata Log Entry: Append a permanent audit record to context/research/errata_log.md matching context/schemas/errata_schema.json.
Source Hierarchy Rule

In any conflict between an AI agent's memory or an aggregator summary and an SEC 10-K/10-Q filing, the primary SEC filing is the sole authoritative ground truth.
