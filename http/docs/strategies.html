Investment Strategy & Principles

Comprehensive framework for risk-managed alpha generation, multi-decade compounding, universe expansion, and disciplined options execution.

1. Strategic Mandate & 20-Year Compounding Objective

The overarching mandate of our investment strategy is to aggressively manage downside risk while maximizing our annualized return on investment (ROI). All portfolio models, stock selection criteria, and options structures are designed around a clear, non-negotiable quantitative performance target:

Core Performance Target & Failure Standard

Target: An annualized return on investment of 20% or higher over a 20-year horizon.

Failure Criterion: We will consider it a failure of our investment strategy if our total annualized return is less than 20% after 20 years of placing trades.

A 20% annualized return over 20 years represents extraordinary compounding power. At a 20% Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR), initial invested capital doubles approximately every 3.8 years, growing by a factor of 38.3x over two decades (turning $100,000 into ~$3.83 million), compared to ~6.7x for a standard 10% broad market baseline.

2. Active US Equities vs. Passive Index & Mutual Funds

We are fully aware of the academic and financial literature regarding passive index funds and mutual funds (e.g. Modern Portfolio Theory, Efficient Market Hypothesis, and low-cost index investing). We understand the merits of passive investing for general market participants. However, we are not buying and holding index funds or mutual funds.

Instead, our strategy is deliberately active and focused on individual security selection:

  • Individual Public Equities: We actively buy and sell shares of individual public companies listed exclusively on US exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ, AMEX).
  • Selective Concentration over Dilution: Broad market index funds hold hundreds or thousands of companies, including unprofitable, highly levered, or structurally declining businesses that dilute long-term portfolio returns. By rigorously filtering for elite business models, we seek concentrated exposure only to high-return assets.
  • Forensic Quality Standards: We focus exclusively on businesses with durable economic moats, high Returns on Invested Capital (ROIC > 15%), strong Free Cash Flow (FCF) conversion, healthy balance sheets, and secular tailwinds.
  • Tactical Asymmetry: Individual equity ownership enables us to selectively purchase shares at deep discounts (Margin of Safety) and harvest idiosyncratic volatility premiums and valuation-anchored options yields that are unattainable on broad index products.

3. Coverage Universe Expansion & Quantitative Market Screening

Rather than remaining restricted to a fixed list of companies, our Equity Research Agent continuously monitors and screens the broader US public markets to expand our coverage universe on demand:

  • Market-Wide Screening: We screen US exchange-listed companies against our quantitative hurdle: conservative 3-year revenue growth and valuation modeling capable of generating ≥ 20% annualized ROI.
  • Stage 1 Lightweight Triage: Candidate equities must demonstrate viable gross margins (≥ 15%), sufficient cash runway (≥ 12 months or positive FCF), prudent leverage (Debt/Equity ≤ 4.0x), and restrained dilution (≤ 4.0%/year).
  • On-Demand Addition: The human trader can request to onboard single or batch equities into coverage at any time (anticipated a few times per year) via scripts/onboard_company.py.
  • Equal Footing: All onboarded equities receive complete institutional thesis dossiers, SEC XBRL modeling, and live tracking on completely equal footing in public intelligence.

5. Entry & Exit Pricing: Fundamental + Technical Synthesis

Determining when to enter a position, what price to pay, and when to exit requires synthesizing both fundamental valuation models and technical price action indicators:

Fundamental Indicators (Valuation Baseline)

Establishes what to buy and fair intrinsic value:

  • 13-Quarter Revenue Forecast: Bottom-up segment-level revenue modeling across 3 full years (Q0 to Q12).
  • 6-Horizon Share Counts: Projections of diluted shares accounting for FCF buybacks vs SBC dilution.
  • Margin of Safety: Requiring a 15% to 25% discount to calculated base target valuation before initiating BUY status.
  • Quality Hurdles: ROIC > 15%, FCF/Net Income > 80%, Net Debt/EBITDA < 2.5×.

Technical Indicators (Execution & Timing)

Refines when to execute and at what limit price:

  • Moving Average Trend Alignment: 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Averages (SMAs) to confirm structural trend regime and avoid catching falling knives.
  • Support & Resistance Zones: Key multi-month horizontal consolidation floors to set put strikes and limit buys; prior breakout peaks for call strikes and limit exits.
  • Momentum & Exhaustion (RSI): 14-period RSI to identify oversold accumulation zones (RSI < 35) and overbought distribution zones (RSI > 70).
  • Volatility Bands (Bollinger / ATR): Measures expected price variance to optimize limit order pricing.

6. Derivatives Strategy: Yield Enhancement & Downside Mitigation

To accelerate our return on investment toward our 20%+ target while simultaneously mitigating downside risk, we systematically sell options across two primary structures:

A. Cash-Secured Puts (CSPs) - Accumulation & Yield Generation

  • Mechanism: We sell Out-of-the-Money (OTM) put options on high-conviction BUY candidates with strike prices set at or below our fundamental Margin of Safety and technical support levels.
  • Target Parameters: 0.15 to 0.30 Delta (80% to 85% probability of expiring OTM) and 30 to 45 Days to Expiration (DTE).
  • Double Advantage:
    • If the option expires OTM, we retain 100% of the upfront premium, generating an Annualized Return on Collateral (AROC) of 12% to 20%+.
    • If the stock is assigned, our net purchase basis equals the strike price minus the collected premium, securing shares at an even deeper discount to fair value.

B. Covered Calls (CCs) - Yield Harvesting & Disciplined Exits

  • Mechanism: We sell OTM call options against existing 100-share equity lots as the stock appreciates toward our calculated base or bull target valuation.
  • Target Parameters: 0.20 to 0.35 Delta and 21 to 45 DTE.
  • Double Advantage:
    • Harvests continuous cash flow (extrinsic time value) while holding high-conviction compounders.
    • Provides an automated, disciplined mechanism to scale out of positions at favorable target prices without emotional hesitation.

C. Buy to Close (BTC) - Downside Risk Elimination on Losing Propositions

  • Mechanism: If we have sold cash-secured puts or covered calls on equities that we determine are losing propositions or undergoing structural thesis invalidation, we buy to close (BTC) the short puts or calls on Monday market open.
  • Short Put Defense: Buying to close short puts on deteriorating companies strictly prevents assignment, eliminating the severe risk of being forced to buy 100 shares of a company trending further down.
  • Short Call Defense: Buying to close short calls on deteriorating positions unlocks the underlying 100-share blocks so the entire equity position can be immediately sold and liquidated at market open.

7. Non-Negotiable Risk Prohibitions & Boundary Rules

Risk management is paramount. To eliminate catastrophic ruin risk and preserve capital for 20-year compounding, the following rules are strictly enforced across all portfolios:

Strict Derivatives Prohibitions
  • NO Speculative Option Buying: We never buy long calls, long puts, or speculative debit spreads for directional bets. Long options suffer from perpetual time decay (Theta drag) and carry a high statistical probability of total loss. (Note: Buying to close existing short positions on losing propositions to avert assignment or liquidate shares is fully permitted and enforced).
  • NO Naked Options: We never sell naked puts or naked calls under any circumstances. Naked options create unbounded liability and margin call risk.
  • 100% Collateralization: Every Cash-Secured Put MUST have 100% cash or SGOV backing the full assignment liability (Strike × 100 × Contracts). Every Covered Call MUST be backed by 100 actual shares owned.
  • NO Margin Debt: Borrowing on margin is strictly prohibited.

9. Execution Cadence: Single-Session "Set-and-Forget"

Our operational model is built specifically for individuals with demanding full-time responsibilities:

  • Single-Session Execution: All order recommendations for the week are formulated over the weekend and entered in one sitting at Monday 9:30 AM ET market open (or as soon as the trader logs in).
  • Zero Mid-Week Babysitting: There is zero intraday monitoring, mid-week order chasing, or manual intervention required.
  • Execution-Time Contingencies: Where market open price movement might alter optimal order parameters, plans provide deterministic execution-time branching (e.g., "If ticker ≥ $X at order entry, submit Limit Order A; if < $X, submit Limit Order B instead").
  • Automated Expiration & Settlement: Expiring Friday options settle automatically with the broker at 4:00 PM ET. Over the weekend, a new brokerage snapshot updates the portfolio state for the next deliberative cycle.
