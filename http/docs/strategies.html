Investment Strategy & Constraints

Core portfolio principles, risk parameters, and boundary rules enforced during every weekly run.

Non-Negotiable Portfolio Constraints

Constraint Specification Rationale
Asset Universe US exchange-listed common stocks only (NYSE, NASDAQ, AMEX). Ensures institutional liquidity, high reporting transparency (SEC EDGAR), and deep options chains.
Cash Proxy Unallocated cash held in SGOV (0-3 Month US Treasury ETF). Generates steady risk-free yield on collateral while maintaining near-cash liquidity for option assignments.
Permitted Derivatives Cash-Secured Puts (CSPs) and Covered Calls (CCs) only. Strictly eliminates ruin risk. No margin borrowing, naked options, or debit spreads.
Position Concentration Maximum of 25 simultaneous active equity positions. Balances meaningful conviction with idiosyncratic company risk mitigation.
Execution Mode Weekly cadence, limit orders only for Monday market open (9:30 AM ET). Eliminates emotional intraday trading and ensures structured execution.

Core Investment Philosophy

The system operates at the intersection of high-quality value investing and disciplined derivatives income generation:

  • Acquire High-Quality Moats at a Discount: Sell Cash-Secured Puts on wonderful companies at strike prices matching conservative Margin of Safety valuations.
  • Monetize Volatility: Harvest option premium over the 30-45 DTE window where time decay (Theta) accelerates most rapidly.
  • Systematic Covered Call Exits: When a stock approaches its fair value target, write Covered Calls on 100-share blocks to collect premium while scaling out systematically.
  • Active Invalidation Discipline: Hold positions with high conviction, but exit decisively when fundamental thesis criteria are violated.
Derivatives Safety Rule

Every Cash-Secured Put MUST have 100% cash or SGOV backing the full assignment liability (Strike x 100 x Contracts). Margin borrowing is strictly prohibited.
