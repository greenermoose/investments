Valuation Framework & Fundamental Analysis

Systematic screening criteria, multi-stage discounted cash flow models, and Owner Earnings valuation.

Fundamental Screening Filters

To qualify as an investable candidate, a company must demonstrate exceptional capital allocation efficiency and durable competitive moats:

Metric Screen Threshold Strategic Purpose
ROIC (3-5 Yr Avg) > 15.0% Ensures superior capital efficiency and economic moat.
Free Cash Flow Conversion FCF / Net Income > 80% Guarantees accounting profits translate to hard cash flows.
Leverage Ratio Net Debt / EBITDA < 2.5x Protects against interest rate shocks and credit stress.
Revenue Growth > 7.0% Organic CAGR Confirms alignment with secular growth tailwinds.

Valuation Models

1. Multi-Stage Discounted Free Cash Flow (DCF)

Projects discrete unlevered free cash flows over a 5-year forecast period, discounted back at the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), plus a perpetual terminal growth value (2.5%-3.0%).

2. Owner Earnings Yield

Owner Earnings = Net Income + D&A - Maintenance CapEx

Measures the cash yield a 100% owner could extract annually without impairing the business's competitive position. We require an Owner Earnings Yield exceeding the 3-Month Treasury yield plus a minimum 300 bps equity risk premium.

Margin of Safety & Price Targets

The 20% Margin of Safety Buffer

Target buy entry prices are set at a 15% to 25% discount to calculated intrinsic fair value. Cash-Secured Put strikes are anchored at or below this Margin of Safety threshold.
