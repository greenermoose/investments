Weekly Workflow & System Operations

Detailed mechanics for weekly plan generation, coverage expansion, scheduled maintenance, and the public research audit trail.

Human workflow and operator workflows

The weekly snapshot-to-plan loop is the routine human workflow. Coverage expansion, market-data refreshes, SEC ingestion, research repair, and full audits are operator workflows that the agent team runs when the repository needs them. See How to Use This Repo for the shorter human checklist.

Workflow 1: Weekly Deliberation & Single-Session Execution

Connecting weekend portfolio ingestion, agent deliberation, executive reporting, and Monday market-open order placement.

1. Weekend Snapshot

Timing: Saturday / Sunday

User places fresh brokerage exports or screenshots into private/snapshots/.

2. Multi-Agent Deliberation

Timing: Sunday Evening

Ingestion parses holdings; Research and Thesis agents refresh evidence and assumptions; Memory audits invalidations; Pricing computes limits.

3. Executive Plan Generation

Timing: Sunday Night

Lead Portfolio Manager writes the weekly trading plan to private/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-plan.txt in human-centric plain text.

4. Monday Execution

Timing: Monday 9:30 AM ET

User reviews the plan and enters specified limit orders into their brokerage in one sitting with zero mid-week intervention.

Sample Plain-Text Weekly Trading Plan Structure

Weekly trading plans written to private/plans/ are strictly plain ASCII text conforming to context/schemas/trading_plan_schema.json:

================================================================================

OBJECTIVE: Maximize probability of achieving >= 20% annualized return over 20 years.
CADENCE:   Single-session "set-and-forget" execution (Monday 9:30 AM ET or as soon
           as you can access the account). No mid-week babysitting or monitoring.
           Friday option settlements occur automatically. Upload a new snapshot
           next weekend to evaluate results and generate the next plan.

Execute all orders for PORTFOLIO 1 first, then proceed to PORTFOLIO 2.

PORTFOLIO 1: PRIMARY GROWTH ACCOUNT
================================================================================

ACCOUNT SNAPSHOT:
- Total Account Value: $121,602.50
- Settled Cash:       $11,500.00
- SGOV (Cash Proxy):  365 shares ($36,682.50)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Submit the following 4 orders in one sitting at market open (or upon first login):

1. SELL TO OPEN: NVDA 09/25/2026 $120.00 Put (Cash-Secured Put)
   - Contracts:   1 (-100 share commitment)
   - Order Type:  Limit Order (Day or GTC)
   - Limit Price: $3.10 credit (or better)

   - Rationale:   Target entry at attractive support or harvest 2.58% 35-day ROI
                  (26.9% annualized) on idle cash collateral.

2. ROLL COVERED CALL: AAPL (Apple Inc)
   - Step A:      BUY TO CLOSE AAPL 09/18/2026 $240.00 Call (Limit $0.85 debit)
   - Step B:      SELL TO OPEN AAPL 09/25/2026 $245.00 Call (Limit $3.85 credit)

   - Limit Price: >= $3.00 net credit ($300.00 cash proceeds)
   - Rationale:   Harvests 81% max profit on near-term leg and lifts strike $5 higher,
                  preserving upside while generating high-conviction yield.

3. CASH-SECURED PUT WITH ORDER-ENTRY CONTINGENCY: AMZN (Amazon.com Inc)

Workflow 2: Coverage Universe Expansion & Onboarding

Enables the Equity Research Agent to proactively search the market, screen for high-conviction companies with the potential to generate >= 20% annualized ROI, and onboard single or batch equities on demand at any time.

1. Discovery & Screening

Equity Research Agent searches US exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ, AMEX) or filters candidates via scripts/screen_market.py for 20%+ ROI compounders.

2. Stage 1 Triage & Solvency

Verifies gross margins >= 15%, cash runway >= 12m, Debt/Equity <= 4.0x, dilution <= 4%/yr, and clean SEC audit opinions.

3. SEC Ingestion & Catalogs

scripts/onboard_company.py registers the ticker, pulls available Tier 1 XBRL facts and market data, and marks a new record AWAITING_RESEARCH. Onboarding does not invent a rating or thesis.

4. Research Store & Derived Dossier

Agents author qualitative judgments and forward-looking parameters under research in context/data/equities/<SYM>.json. Deterministic engines calculate outputs and render the derived thesis and web data.

6-Stage End-to-End Onboarding Pipeline

  1. Exchange & Security Validation: Verifies common stock or liquid ADR listed on NYSE, NASDAQ, or AMEX. Excludes OTC penny stocks, closed-end funds, and leveraged ETFs.
  2. The 20%+ ROI Hurdle & Stage 1 Triage: Quantitatively computes estimated 3-year compounding rate. Requires estimated CAGR >= 20.0%. Discards value traps to the Avoid List.
  3. Deterministic Data Ingestion: Registers the symbol and ingests available SEC EDGAR XBRL facts and market data. A new symbol remains AWAITING_RESEARCH.
  4. Agent Research Authoring: Agents verify sources and write the business profile, market opportunity, moat, catalysts, invalidations, revenue assumptions, share-count assumptions, and valuation parameters into the research store.
  5. Deterministic Modeling & Rendering: Valuation and return engines calculate the 13-quarter revenue path, 6-horizon share path, 4-horizon valuation ranges, rating outputs, thesis dossier, and public web artifacts from researched inputs.
  6. Parity & Quality Control Audit: Schema validators and scripts/quality_control.py --audit reject missing research, inconsistent derived files, and other data errors.

How to Invoke Universe Expansion

You can ask the AI agent team to expand the coverage universe at any time using one of three prompt patterns:

Pattern A: Thematic / Sector Market Screening for 20%+ Compounders

Use when you want the Equity Research Agent to proactively discover new candidates in a specific sector or theme:

Act as the Equity Research Agent. Search the public markets and SEC filings for high-growth cybersecurity and enterprise cloud infrastructure companies listed on US exchanges that offer high potential to achieve >= 20% annualized ROI.

Run Stage 1 Lightweight Triage (gross margin >= 15%, runway >= 12m, Debt/Equity <= 4.0x, dilution <= 4%/yr), audit balance sheet solvency and off-balance sheet liabilities, and select the top 3 highest-conviction candidates.

Onboard these equities into our universe by executing scripts/onboard_company.py with live regulatory data, author complete thesis dossiers with the Investment Thesis Agent, validate schema compliance, and execute scripts/quality_control.py --audit. Provide a summary of newly added equities with expected CAGR and dossier paths.

Pattern B: Single Named Public Equity Onboarding

Use when you have a specific US-listed company in mind that you want to add to full coverage:

Act as the Equity Research Agent. Add CRWD to our coverage universe.

Ingest authoritative SEC EDGAR 10-K/10-Q filings and live market prices using python scripts/onboard_company.py --symbol CRWD --live. Evaluate Stage 1 triage and solvency. Collaborate with the Investment Thesis Agent to author the full 6-part qualitative thesis in context/theses/CRWD.md, model the 13Q revenue trajectory and 4H price targets, validate schema compliance with scripts/validate_thesis.py, and run scripts/quality_control.py --audit.

Pattern C: Batch Multi-Equity Onboarding (Any Number of Additions)

Use when adding any arbitrary list of companies simultaneously (e.g. during quarterly universe refresh):

Act as the Equity Research Agent. Add the following 4 equities to our coverage universe: NOW, ABNB, NET, MDB.

Execute the batch onboarding pipeline via python scripts/onboard_company.py --symbols NOW ABNB NET MDB --live. Ensure SEC XBRL statements, market quotes, analyst price targets, and master catalogs (universe.json and sec-data.json) are fully synchronized. Author and validate thesis dossiers for each company, and assert zero discrepancies with scripts/quality_control.py --audit.

Deterministic CLI Commands for Universe Onboarding

These commands perform ingestion, screening, and validation. They do not replace the agent-authored research step:

# 1. Onboard a single named equity (live SEC & market data)
python scripts/onboard_company.py --symbol CRWD --live

# 2. Onboard multiple equities in batch (any arbitrary list)
python scripts/onboard_company.py --symbols NOW ABNB NET MDB --live

# 3. Screen market for high-conviction >= 20% ROI candidates and auto-onboard top N
python scripts/onboard_company.py --screen --min-roi 20.0 --sector Technology --limit 3

# 4. Screen existing universe for >= 20% annualized ROI (excluding Avoid list)
python scripts/screen_market.py --min-roi 20.0 --exclude-avoid

# 5. Screen full universe categorization summary across BUY, HOLD, SELL, and AVOID
python scripts/screen_market.py --summary

# 6. Audit quality control across all universe records after onboarding
python scripts/quality_control.py --audit
Equal-Footing & Privacy Rules for Newly Onboarded Equities

All equities added to the coverage universe exist on completely equal footing in public intelligence (context/theses/*.md, http/data/universe.json, and the web interface). Private portfolio holdings in private/snapshots/ NEVER alter public ratings or dossiers, preserving the strict one-way privacy firewall.

Repository Maintenance Cadences

This material is for the agent or maintainer operating the research system. A human requesting a weekly plan should not need to run each tool manually. The unified command reference is available from python scripts/manage_universe.py --help.

Cadence Purpose Primary entry point Expected result
Frequent Refresh prices, volume, moving averages, and 52-week ranges. manage_universe.py update-prices Updated public market-price datasets.
Weekly Parse new private snapshots and produce the dated Monday plan. weekly_deliberation.md and render_plan.py Validated private plan, isolated by account.
Event-driven Record and assess observed news, sentiment, short reports, or thesis invalidations. Relevant agent prompt and surveillance readers. Source-attributed observations and thesis action when warranted.
Scheduled Anticipate and ingest new 10-Q and 10-K filings. manage_universe.py refresh-sec Fresh regulatory facts and identified research gaps.
On demand Screen, onboard, and fully research additional equities. onboard_company.py plus research authoring and rendering. Validated research-store record and derived public artifacts.
Rare audit Rebuild and reconcile the public intelligence layer from authoritative sources. manage_universe.py rebuild-all Reconciled datasets, quality-control results, and logged corrections.
Scripts calculate; agents author

Mathematical and rule-based work belongs in deterministic scripts. Company narratives, forward-looking assumptions, ratings rationale, and trade rationale must be authored by agents into the designated research or order schema. A missing researched field is a gap, not permission for a script to invent a default.

Audit Trail and Cross-Run Memory

The system maintains directory-based audit logs for institutional continuity across agent sessions:

  • Agent run logs: context/research/runs/{run_id}.json - one file per generative session, managed via activity_ledger.py.
  • Errata registry: context/research/errata/{erratum_id}.json - one file per verified correction, managed via errata_log.py.
  • Generative audits: context/research/audits/*.md - prose deliverables linked from run files.
