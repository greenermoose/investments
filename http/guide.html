User Guide & Operational Cadence

Step-by-step instructions for operating the multi-agent investment advisory system on a disciplined, single-session weekly schedule.

Core Operating Philosophy: Single-Session "Set-and-Forget"

This system is specifically engineered for investors with demanding full-time professional schedules. There is zero mid-week babysitting or intraday monitoring. All deliberation takes place over the weekend, all limit orders are entered in one sitting on Monday morning, and all Friday option expirations settle automatically.

How to Operate the System: Step-by-Step

Follow this 5-step operational protocol every week to run deliberation, review your personalized plan, and execute trades.

Step 1

Capture Weekend Portfolio Snapshots

Export your account holdings as a CSV or capture a clean screenshot from your brokerage portal. Save the file directly into the git-ignored private directory:

private/snapshots/2026-08-15-schwab.csv
private/snapshots/2026-08-15-fidelity.png

If managing multiple accounts (e.g. taxable growth + retirement), drop snapshots for each account into private/snapshots/. The agents will parse and generate separate, self-contained order blocks for each portfolio.

Step 2

Launch IDE or AI Agent Harness

Open your AI coding assistant (such as Antigravity IDE, Claude Code, or Cursor) pointed directly at this repository root. This provides the AI agents with immediate access to repository guidelines (AGENTS.md), schemas, prompts, and public thesis dossiers.

Step 3

Execute the Weekly Deliberation Protocol

Prompt your AI agent to ingest the snapshots and trigger the deliberation pipeline using the prompt in context/prompts/weekly_deliberation.md:

Please ingest private/snapshots/ and run context/prompts/weekly_deliberation.md to generate our Monday execution plan.

The specialized sub-agents will automatically parse share counts, analyze thesis catalysts, screen candidate equities, calculate Black-Scholes option pricing, and synthesize decisions into a cohesive plan.

Step 4

Review the Decisive Trading Plan

Open the generated plan in private/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-plan.txt. The plan is rendered in clean, plain ASCII text conforming to repository schemas:

  • Account Snapshot: Total Value, Cash, SGOV proxy, Dry Powder percentage, and Active Positions.
  • Monday 9:30 AM Orders: Clear numbered list of Limit Orders with exact limit prices, cash impacts, and rationales.
  • Execution-Time Contingencies: Deterministic price branches (e.g. Branch A if stock >= $X; Branch B if stock < $X) for frictionless entry.
  • Friday Expiration Expectations: Clear forecast of automated option settlements and collateral unlocks.
Step 5

Monday Morning Execution

Log in to your brokerage at 9:30 AM ET (or whenever you can access your account Monday morning) and submit all specified limit orders in one sitting. Once entered, log out. No further actions or mid-week checks are required until next weekend's snapshot.

Key Execution Rules & Principles

Zero Ambiguity Policy

The system never presents open-ended dilemmas or "choose X or Y depending on risk tolerance". Every position receives a single, high-conviction recommendation.

Deterministic Contingencies

If market conditions fluctuate at market open, the plan provides instant deterministic branching (Branch A vs Branch B) so you never need to improvise.

Multi-Portfolio Isolation

Distinct accounts are analyzed independently. Orders for Portfolio 1 are executed completely before moving to Portfolio 2.

Automated Friday Settlement

Option expirations, call assignments, and cash collateral releases occur automatically via your broker at 4:00 PM ET on Friday.
