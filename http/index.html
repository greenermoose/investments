Agentic Investment Advisor

A multi-agent equity advisory system designed to maximize the probability of achieving a 20%+ annualized return over a 20-year horizon.

System Architecture Principle

This repository is not a point-and-click web application. The core engine is executed inside your IDE or AI harness pointed at this repository, orchestrating specialized AI sub-agents with full repository context over your private portfolio snapshots and public equity theses.

How to Operate the System

Operating this system follows a weekly single-session cadence designed for traders with demanding full-time schedules. There is zero mid-week monitoring or babysitting.

Step 1

Capture Weekend Portfolio Snapshots

Export your account holdings as a CSV or capture a screenshot from your brokerage, and place the file in the git-ignored private directory:

private/snapshots/2026-08-15-schwab.csv
private/snapshots/2026-08-15-fidelity.png
Step 2

Open IDE or AI Agent Harness

Launch your AI coding assistant (e.g. Antigravity IDE, Claude Code, Cursor) opened directly at this repository root so it has complete access to repository rules, schemas, and public theses.

Step 3

Run the Weekly Deliberation Protocol

Prompt your AI agent with the master deliberation prompt located at context/prompts/weekly_deliberation.md:

Please ingest private/snapshots/ and run context/prompts/weekly_deliberation.md to generate our Monday execution plan.
Step 4

Review Decisive Trading Plan

The AI agents parse holdings, evaluate thesis catalysts, model Black-Scholes options pricing, and write a human-readable plain text trading plan in private/plans/ with unambiguous Monday limit orders.

Step 5

Monday 9:30 AM ET Single-Session Execution

Place the specified Limit Orders at market open. All Friday options expirations, cash assignments, and call-aways settle automatically at 4:00 PM ET with zero mid-week intervention.

Multi-Agent Architecture

Rather than relying on a single generalist chatbot, the system coordinates five specialized roles backed by deterministic data structures:

1. Portfolio Ingestion Agent

Extracts holdings, share lots, cash balances, SGOV shares, and open options contracts from raw snapshots, identifying covered call eligibility.

2. Thesis & Memory Agent

Maintains persistent markdown dossiers in context/theses/, tracking multi-year catalysts, thesis health, and flagging broken theses for exit.

3. Universe Screener & Analyst

Screens the US public equity universe for high-ROIC, positive-FCF compounders while strictly enforcing the 25-position portfolio ceiling.

4. Derivatives Specialist

Models Black-Scholes pricing over the weekend to compute precise Monday limit orders for Cash-Secured Puts, Covered Calls, and credit rolls.

5. Lead Portfolio Manager

Synthesizes sub-agent analysis into an unambiguous, single-conviction Weekly Trading Plan in private/plans/.

Core Risk Rules & Portfolio Constraints

Rule Constraint Implementation Details
Asset Universe US Public Equities Exchange-listed US companies and ADRs (NYSE, NASDAQ, AMEX). No mutual funds or broad equity ETFs.
Cash Proxy SGOV Only iShares 0-3 Month Treasury Bond ETF is the sole allowed ETF, holding idle cash at risk-free yield.
Derivatives CSPs & CCs Only Strictly no naked options. All puts must be 100% cash-secured; all calls must be covered by >= 100 shares.
Rolling Rules Defensive & Income Rolls (out/down for puts, out/up for calls) are executed strictly for a net credit.
Concentration Max 25 Positions Strict cap of 25 holdings across the portfolio to ensure deep research depth and high conviction.
Cadence Single-Session Weekly Weekend analysis and planning; set-and-forget limit orders placed Monday at 9:30 AM ET.
