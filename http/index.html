Orchestration & User Experience

This repository is not a point-and-click web application. Prompt your AI models in your IDE or harness pointed at this repository to orchestrate specialized AI sub-agents with context that includes your private portfolio snapshots, public equity theses, and deterministic scripts.

System Rationale: Why Agent Teams Outperform Monolithic Chatbots

Standard LLM chat sessions fail at reliable, personalized portfolio management due to four fundamental architectural flaws:

  • Session Amnesia: Ephemeral chat contexts lose track of historical cost bases, catalyst milestones, invalidation triggers, and multi-week option roll chains.
  • Guardrail Hedging & Indecision: Generic consumer bots produce timid, non-actionable answers filled with open-ended "it depends on your risk tolerance" disclaimers.
  • Narrow Universe Focus: Single prompt windows lack the context capacity to simultaneously audit hundreds of public equities against verified SEC filings.
  • Absence of Deterministic Derivatives Modeling: Standard language models cannot reliably calculate Black-Scholes Greeks, volatility skews, or exact limit pricing formulas.

This system resolves these limitations by breaking the advisory process into six discrete, specialized sub-agent roles backed by persistent file-based memory, strict data provenance, forward-looking quantitative forecasting, and deterministic Python calculation scripts.

Multi-Agent Sub-Agent Pipeline

This repo encourages AI to coordinate six specialized roles backed by structured data contracts:

1. Portfolio Ingestion Agent

Input: Brokerage screenshot or CSV export in private/snapshots/.

Responsibility: Extracts share counts, options contracts, cash balances, and SGOV shares. Identifies 100-share blocks eligible for Covered Call income generation while maintaining strict multi-account isolation.

2. Equity Research Agent

Input: The Internet, US public exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ, AMEX), SEC EDGAR NPORT-P / 10-K filings, and screening tools.

Responsibility: Proactively searches public markets to discover high-potential companies, verifies solvency and runway, screens for high probability of achieving >= 20% annualized ROI, and onboards single or batch equities into our coverage universe on demand.

3. Investment Thesis Agent

Input: Tracked universe of equities backed by Tier 1 SEC EDGAR filings (10-K/10-Q) and earnings releases.

Responsibility: Builds institutional 3-year quantitative forecasts (13-quarter revenue path, 6-horizon shares outstanding, 4-horizon price target bounds), authors detailed Revenue and P/S Multiple narratives, and assigns decisive BUY, HOLD, SELL, or AVOID ratings.

4. Memory Agent

Tools: manage_memory.py, activity_ledger.py, errata_log.py.

Reads: Persistent dossiers in context/theses/*.md, agent run logs in context/research/runs/, errata registry in context/research/errata/, and context/research/errata_protocol.md.

Responsibility: Manages institutional context across runs, audits catalyst execution against deadlines, monitors explicit invalidation exit triggers, maintains the errata registry, and issues urgent liquidation alerts for broken theses.

5. Pricing Agent

Input: Valuation targets from Thesis Agent, technical price structures, moving averages, and volatility surfaces.

Responsibility: Predicts price trends to calculate exact limit orders for common stocks, models Black-Scholes options pricing for Cash-Secured Puts (0.15-0.30 Delta) and Covered Calls (0.20-0.35 Delta), and verifies net-credit rolls.

6. Lead Portfolio Manager

Input: Sub-agent recommendations and portfolio risk constraints.

Responsibility: Synthesizes executive decisions into an unambiguous plain-text Weekly Trading Plan (private/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-plan.txt) with single-session Monday execution.

Data Isolation & Privacy Architecture

The repository strictly isolates private portfolio data from public equity intelligence:

  • context/ and http/ contain 100% public, non-confidential data (SEC filings, market quotes, public theses).
  • private/snapshots/ and private/plans/ are strictly local and git-ignored. Private account numbers, trade quantities, and balances never leak into shared files or repositories.
  • context/research/runs/ and context/research/errata/ record public agent activity and verified corrections only. Run logs and errata never contain private portfolio data.

Core Investment Strategy Principles

Strategy Dimension Standard & Parameter Implementation Details
Return Hurdle ≥ 20% Annualized ROI All equity selections, cash-secured puts, and covered calls must maximize the probability of achieving ≥ 20% annual return over a 20-year horizon.
Asset Focus US Public Equities Individual common stocks and liquid ADRs listed on NYSE, NASDAQ, and AMEX. Strictly no mutual funds or broad ETFs (except SGOV for cash).
Cash Management SGOV Yield Proxy Idle dry powder is parked in SGOV (0-3 Month Treasury ETF) earning risk-free yields while collateralizing Cash-Secured Puts.
Derivatives Discipline 100% Collateralized & BTC Strictly NO naked options and NO long speculative options. Puts are 100% cash-secured; calls are 100% covered. Buy to Close (BTC) is used to eliminate downside on losing propositions.
Execution Cadence Single-Session Monday Zero mid-week babysitting. All limit orders are entered Monday 9:30 AM ET; Friday option settlements occur automatically.
